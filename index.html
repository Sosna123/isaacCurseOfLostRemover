CURSE OF LOST REMOVER

This is a mapper for The Binding Of Isaac

  • Pick a room on the left menu and click on the map to place it
  • Use right click to clear the room
  • Hold left click to place big rooms (uses the image selected, there are no borders inside big rooms)
  • Hold right click to clear more rooms
  • Use the "P" button while hovering a map room, to change progress. There are 4 modes available:
    • No color - room fully cleared
    • Orange - something is left to do (pickups, tinted rock etc.)
    • Red - room uncleared
  • Press arrows to move the whole map one space to the pressed direction (removes rooms "out of bounds")
